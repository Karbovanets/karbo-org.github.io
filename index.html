About

You are your own bank.

Karbo is people’s electronic cash: a peer-to-peer exchange medium that can be used without intermediaries, without account opening procedures, and without asking permission. Download a wallet, hold your own keys, and use the network directly.

Open and accessible

Karbo is an open and decentralized network. Everyone is welcome to participate in usage, mining, development, and governance.

Direct control

Only you control your money and are responsible for its safety. The system operates according to a predefined mathematical algorithm without central control bodies.

Peer-to-peer

Transactions take place through a decentralized system without intermediaries. That is the starting point, not a feature add-on.

Core Values

Built on principles not promises.

We built Karbo on principles of decentralization, privacy, and egalitarian participation. Those principles guide our design choices and development priorities, and they are more important to us than any specific feature or roadmap item.

Signed Proof-of-Work

One CPU —
One Vote.

Pool mining centralizes power.

ASIC mining centralizes hardware.

Centralization opens the door to censorship.

Centralization undermines the principles of decentralization and egalitarian participation.

One of the key principles of CryptoNote is Egalitarian Proof of Work: the POW mechanism acts as a voting system, so participants should have equal voting privileges. We do not think mining centralization serves that principle. When miners join pools, they surrender every meaningful decision: which transactions to include, which chain to follow, which proposals to support. A handful of pool operators gain control — and, for instance, can be pressured by regulators to censor transactions.

Karbo's Signed Proof-of-Work makes this structurally impossible. We introduced CPU-only solo mining approach using custom YeSPOWer, blockchain cache, and signing blocks with miners’ private keys. The purpose is to make pooled mining harder and thus to reduce pool and botnet centralization and to keep more regular users solo-mining and validating their own chain view.

Heritage

A name
carved in
history

Karbovanets (карбованець) — the historic name for Ukraine's currency, from the word meaning to notch or to carve a tally.

The karbovanets has deep roots in Ukrainian monetary history. The word itself comes from the ancient practice of carving notches into tally sticks to record debts and transactions — a pre-bank, pre-institution system of keeping accounts that belonged to the people who made the marks.

Karbo carries this heritage forward. Where tally sticks were carved wood, Karbo is carved mathematics. The ledger is distributed across thousands of machines. The marks cannot be erased, altered, or confiscated. The history is the record, and the record belongs to no one.

“Karbovanets” was also a distinct unit of currency in Ukraine during different periods of its history. We brought that name back in digital form as an unofficial and independent exchange medium.
